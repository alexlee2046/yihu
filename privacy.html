隐私政策

适用于 iOS App「一呼 Yihu」及其分享扩展。发布者 Guangdong Mifeng Intelligent Technology Co., Ltd.。生效日期 2026-10-06。

一呼本身

一呼没有自己的账号系统，不出售个人信息。你连接的工作台由该工作台的运营者处理，不由本政策概括。

保存在手机上的内容

你填写的工作台地址和本机偏好保存在设备上。内置浏览器按每个工作台来源分开保存网站数据，例如 Cookie。可选的语音模型保存在 App 数据中，并排除 iCloud 备份。

语音

短语音由本机模型处理，结果先进入草稿，不会自动发送。长语音笔记先在本机保存和转写；只有你明确交给工作台时，文字或音频才会发到你选中的工作台。下载可选模型时，手机会访问你选择的 ModelScope 或 Hugging Face，这些网站按它们自己的规则看到普通网络请求。

如果开启「只认我的声音」，App 从录音得到声纹并只保存在本机，用于填入前比对。可在该设置里删除。声纹不会上传，也不是账号登录。

分享

从其他 App 分享到一呼时，内容先复制到本机收件箱，此时不上传。只有你在 App 里明确插入或附加，才会交给选中的工作台。未完成项会留在本机，直到 App 或你移除。

通知

只有你为兼容的 Collie 工作台开启原生通知时，注册才会把 APNs 设备令牌、安装标识、该次注册凭证、推送环境和主题发给该工作台。若服务器暂时无法注销，注册最长可能保留七天。另有一项仅对已保存的私人工作台显示的提醒，不是面向所有用户的功能；它会把设备标识和相关推送令牌发给该私人中继。这些数据由对应工作台或中继按其自己的规则保存，一呼未核对其保存期限。

儿童与变更

一呼不面向儿童。政策变更会更新本页和生效日期。问题见技术支持。

Privacy Policy

For the iOS app Yihu and its share extension. Publisher: Guangdong Mifeng Intelligent Technology Co., Ltd. Effective 2026-10-06.

Yihu itself

Yihu has no account system of its own and does not sell personal information. A workspace you connect is operated by that workspace, not covered by this policy.

What stays on your iPhone

Saved workspace addresses and native preferences stay on the device. The embedded browser keeps site data, such as cookies, separately for each workspace. An optional speech model is stored in the app's data and excluded from iCloud backup.

Voice

Short dictation is processed on device and inserted into a draft. It is not sent automatically. Longer voice notes are saved and transcribed locally; text or audio is sent to the workspace you selected only when you explicitly hand it over. If you download the optional model, your iPhone contacts the ModelScope or Hugging Face host you choose. Those hosts may see ordinary network requests under their own terms.

If you turn on “only my voice,” the app derives a voiceprint and stores it only on the device to compare before insertion. You can delete it in that setting. The voiceprint is not uploaded and is not an account login.

Share

Sharing into Yihu copies the item into a local inbox and does not upload it at that time. It reaches the selected workspace only if you explicitly insert or attach it. Unfinished items remain on the device until the app or you remove them.

Notifications

Only if you enable native notifications for a compatible Collie workspace does registration send an APNs device token, installation identifier, registration secret, push environment and topic to that workspace. A registration may remain for up to seven days if the server cannot be reached to unregister. A separate reminder appears only for an already saved private workbench and is not a service for every user; it sends a device identifier and the related push tokens to that private relay. Retention by the workspace or relay is not verified by Yihu.

Children and changes

Yihu is not directed at children. If this policy changes, this page and its effective date will be updated. Questions: Support.
