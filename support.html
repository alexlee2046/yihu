技术支持

如何连接

  1. 打开一呼，填入你已有的兼容工作台地址。
  2. 按该工作台自己的方式完成访问。
  3. 需要语音时，在 App 内下载可选语音模型。识别在本机完成，发送前可以改文字。

常见问题

没有内置工作台？ 一呼不提供托管账号。你需要自己的兼容工作台。

语音会自动发出去吗？ 不会。结果先进入草稿，由你发送。

声纹存在哪里？ 只在本机。可在「只认我的声音」里删除。详见隐私政策。

反馈

请在 GitHub Issues 提交问题，附上 iOS 版本和复现步骤。不要附上账号、密码或私人对话。

Support

Connect

  1. Open Yihu and enter the address of a compatible workspace you already use.
  2. Complete access the way that workspace requires.
  3. For voice, download the optional speech model in the app. Recognition runs on your iPhone, and you can edit the text before sending.

FAQ

Is a workspace included? No. Yihu does not provide a hosted account.

Does voice send by itself? No. Text goes into a draft until you send it.

Where is the voiceprint? Only on your iPhone. Delete it under “only my voice.” See the Privacy Policy.

Feedback

Open an issue on GitHub Issues with your iOS version and steps. Do not include account credentials or private conversations.
